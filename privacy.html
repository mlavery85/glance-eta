Privacy Policy

Glance ETA for Mac and iPhone · Effective November 11, 2026

Glance ETA is built to work without collecting your personal information. We don't have accounts, servers, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Location

If you allow it, Glance ETA uses your device's location to calculate travel times from where you are. Your location is used on your device and sent only to Apple Maps (Apple's MapKit service) to calculate routes and travel times. We never receive or store it. Apple handles that data under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Your places and settings

The places you save and your settings are stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, they are also stored in your own iCloud account so your devices stay in sync. We can't access your iCloud data.

Purchases

Glance ETA Pro subscriptions and the Lifetime purchase are handled by the App Store. Apple processes payments and manages subscriptions; we never see your payment details.

Notifications

Leave-by reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification content leaves your device.

Data we collect

None. We don't collect, sell, or share any personal data, and we don't use third-party analytics or advertising tools.

Children

Glance ETA doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
