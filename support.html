Glance ETA Support

Live travel times to Home and your favorite places, for Mac and iPhone.

Need help or have an idea? Email GlanceETA@proton.me and include your device and macOS/iOS version.

My widget is empty or says to add places

Open Glance ETA and add at least one place. Mark places as favorites (the star) to choose which ones the widget shows. Widgets refresh on a schedule set by the system, so give them a minute after a change.

Travel times aren't showing

Make sure Location Services is on for Glance ETA (System Settings or Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services), or choose a fixed starting address in the app's settings. An internet connection is required.

How is the traffic delay calculated?

Glance ETA compares the current travel time from Apple Maps with the time for the same trip in light traffic. Apple doesn't provide accident or incident details to apps.

I have Pro but it isn't unlocked

Open Settings → Pro and choose Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe or buy Lifetime. One subscription or purchase unlocks Pro on both Mac and iPhone.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

In Glance ETA, open Settings → Pro → Manage Subscription. You can also go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on iPhone, or App Store → your account → Subscriptions on Mac. Pro stays on until the end of the period you've paid for.

My places aren't syncing

iCloud sync is part of Pro. Turn it on in Settings → Pro on each device, and make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive enabled.

Leave-by reminders aren't arriving

Allow notifications for Glance ETA. Reminders use the latest travel time, so open the app occasionally (or keep it in your Mac's menu bar) so times stay current.
